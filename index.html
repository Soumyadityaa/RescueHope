Our Mission

Addressing the core needs of urban animals through dedicated community action.

Rescue

Rapid response to animals suffering from accidents, injuries, abandonment, or unsafe environments.

Providing daily nutritious food and clean drinking water stations to strays in need.

Medical Care

Assisting injured and sick animals to receive professional veterinary treatment and vaccinations.

Shelter

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how we operate and how you can help.

How does my donation help?
Your contribution directly funds animal food, emergency medical treatments, shelter maintenance, and rescue operations. We dedicate 85% of all funds directly to animal care programs.
Can I adopt an animal if I live in an apartment?
Yes! Many of our rescued cats and smaller dogs adapt wonderfully to apartment living. Our team will interview you to ensure the animal's temperament matches your living situation.
Do you rescue animals outside the city limits?
Currently, our primary focus is within urban and immediate suburban areas due to the range of our animal ambulance. However, we coordinate with partner shelters for remote cases.
